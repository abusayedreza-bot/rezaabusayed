Section 03

Localization Philosophy

Four core principles that guide every translation decision.

🎯

Meaning Over Literalism

I translate what a character means, not just what they say. Korean emotional expression often uses indirect phrasing that sounds cold when translated word-for-word. The goal is always to preserve emotional intent for the player.

🔖

Terminology Consistency

From legal interpretation work, I know that inconsistent terminology destroys trust. I build and maintain glossaries and style guides to ensure every term, name, and title stays consistent across an entire game.

🎮

Korean Market Awareness

Korean gamers are sophisticated consumers with high standards for localization quality. I monitor Korean game reviews, community forums, and localization discussions to stay current on what Korean players accept and reject.

🗣️

Voice Script Sensitivity

Written Korean and spoken Korean are very different. Dialogue scripts for voice acting require rhythm, natural pauses, and correct speech register. I write with the voice actor's delivery in mind, not just the reader's eye.
